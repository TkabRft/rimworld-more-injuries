# NPC combat medicine

code-synced: 2026-10-20

Status: implemented. Compilation and automated contracts target the pinned RimWorld 1.6 API. The shared implementation is selected for 1.7 as well; live 1.6/1.7 acceptance is still required. Metadata and packaging do not establish tested 1.7 support. No new dependency or DLC requirement is added.

Player documentation: [medical devices](wiki/medical-devices.md). Shared treatment selection: [medical runtime](medical-runtime.md). Optional integration boundaries: [compatibility](../COMPATIBILITY.md).

## Ownership and lifecycle

`AI/NpcCombatMedicine/` owns profiles, generation, decisions, jobs, settings UI, optional adapters and dose history. `Defs/Internal/NpcMedicalProfiles.xml` provides five `NpcMedicalProfileDef` records. `NpcMedicalProfileExtension.profile` overrides the profile on a pawn kind, then faction; otherwise the actual faction technology selects a default. Undefined/Animal technology uses Neolithic, Archotech uses Ultra. Profile fields remain XML interfaces, independent of colony research.

The inserted humanlike think node handles living fleshy NPC combatants, armed visitors and prisoners. Player-controlled colonists, colony slaves and nonhuman actors are excluded. Medical capability is checked separately: incapable doctors may carry kits and take a permissible performance drug. Prisoners may self-treat; movement uses ordinary reachability, forbidden areas and doors. Withdrawal/evacuation ownership requires an existing prison break for a prisoner. The module never starts breaks, unlocks confinement or changes faction ownership.

Medical-only mode adds treatment without movement decisions. The default treatment-aware mode adds safer positions within 12 cells and an estimated 600-tick travel budget. Full survival also permits individual medical withdrawal and off-map evacuation of nearby downed faction members. Recognized ordinary raid theft/exit phases receive the bounded rescue-before-loot assessment below; this does not order the group to retreat. Existing transport, kidnapping, other departures and ritual duties retain ownership. Completing a temporary treatment/positioning job returns control to ordinary duties.

Patient searches use spatial queries within 12 cells, or 24 for medics. Arrest, critical airway failure, shock, impaired breathing/pumping and dangerous blood loss lead triage; comparable emergencies prefer own faction/group, then allies/player colonists, then neutrals. Cross-faction care requires an emergency. All four care flags default on; self-care is independent of the own-faction flag. Current pawn hostility is checked, including during jobs. Player medical-care restrictions and mutant medical incompatibilities apply. A prisoner's own care may override their assigned medical-care category without overriding physical access.

`NpcMedicalTriage` examines active conditions separately from native tendability. Choking on blood has `<tendable>false>` and must enter triage even with no bleeding or ordinary tendable wounds. It receives urgency 70 from onset, 80 at severity 0.45, and 90 when breathing falls to 20% or less; arrest retains 100. Blood loss and hemodilution above the shared 0.449 transfusion boundary remain emergencies after bleeding stops. Native currently life-threatening stages, heart attack and severe capacity impairment also enter triage without implying that a field cure exists. Applied tourniquets retain a routine aftercare signal after their wound is tended.

| Condition path audited on 2026-10-20 | Current NPC behavior |
|---|---|
| Isolated blood choking, including early stages | Consider suction independently of CPR eligibility; another capable pawn can provide CPR when suction is unavailable. A capable NPC can use suction on itself, but cannot perform CPR on itself. |
| Arrest or heart attack | Retain existing physiological reversibility, defibrillator stage limits and clinical epinephrine safety checks; no field repair of a nonfunctional heart. |
| Blood loss after bleeding has stopped; hemodilution | Consider safe blood/hemogen or saline through existing transfusion indication/count checks, including feasible self-administration. Hemodilution alone does not authorize saline. |
| Hypovolemic shock | Recognize shock independently of ordinary tending. Clinical epinephrine still requires its existing indication, setting and repeat/dose safety checks; fluids still require a genuine safely treatable volume deficit. |
| Fractures and applied tourniquets | Keep existing splinting. Once threats are absent, attempt existing gradual tourniquet removal only after bleeding is controlled and no untreated tourniquet wound remains. Temporary suppression alone is insufficient. |
| Neurological, respiratory and organ failure | Current life-threatening stages or severe capacity impairment affect triage and Full survival decisions; treat an available underlying cause, without inventing a surgery-free cure. |

The choking fix closes an eligibility failure before the shared selector was ever reached. Source-linked regressions cover isolated non-tendable choking across its stages, actual suction clearance, self-care, disabled CPR/research, reservation/care/capability rejection, isolated volume deficits and their exact clinical threshold, heart attack, uncured life-threatening conditions and completed tourniquet aftercare with the untreated-wound safeguard. The pinned Release build, 206 runtime/definition/localization contracts and 24 Harmony/transfer fixtures pass. Live job scheduling, physical retrieval and the full mod patch chain still require game acceptance; 1.7 runtime acceptance is not inferred from these results.

`EmergencyCare.NpcContext` reuses MI's weighted emergency selector without colony research. It supplies capability flags, a bounded item resolver and a separate clinical-drug gate. The existing colonist/drafted caller path remains the default context. Bleeding control, transfusion, CPR, defibrillation, suction, epinephrine, splints and native tending use existing medical behavior. Internal bleeding, dilution and organ/neurological impairment affect urgency and feasible care; surgery-only conditions receive no invented field cure. Routine decisions do not invoke the numerical prognosis simulator.

Decisions and local threat snapshots are throttled to 150 ticks. Supplies are sought in doctor/patient inventories, permitted nearby same-faction inventories, then reachable ground items within 12 cells. Collection uses physical retrieval and reservations, including native tending from another pawn's inventory. Jobs revalidate their targets; failed reservations/retrieval return control to normal AI.

`NpcMedicalReservations` applies the same exclusive patient availability check to self-care and other-pawn care. An NPC receiving treatment must not start competing self-treatment; managed self-device jobs also reserve their patient. Existing native tending jobs retain ownership rather than being interrupted by a new MI decision. CE's tactical manager calls `StartJob` directly, so the adapter performs `Job.TryMakePreToilReservations` quietly before returning an MI job. Failed preflight releases only that proposed job's claims, leaving the current job and other doctors' reservations intact. No job is started just to discover a predictable reservation failure.

`JobDriver_NpcTend` owns a bounded, single-application collection/positioning sequence and retains native `Toils_Tend.FinalizeTend` for tending quality, medicine consumption, experience and health callbacks. It reserves one dose and transfers it directly from the selected inventory into the doctor's carry tracker; ground supplies still require physical collection. It does not invoke native medicine collection or Smart Medicine's globally rewritten pickup toil, and it does not stage inventory medicine on the ground. Standing NPC patients can be treated in place without requiring bed-only laying status. A temporary patient wait is released only if it is still the exact wait this application created; player-controlled patients receive no forced wait. Changed inventory ownership, failed transfer, unrelated cargo, lost capability, hostility or care restrictions abort safely.

Unused selected medicine or medical devices still in the carry tracker return to the pawn's inventory on cleanup, up to remaining mass/bulk capacity. This does not create medicine, reclaim items already dropped on the ground, take unrelated carried cargo or bypass capacity; overflow retains normal cleanup behavior. No new save keys or Harmony targets are required for this collection path.

NPC medical travel has a 2,500-tick deadline measured from the existing job start tick, checked during movement rather than during the medical application. It covers native tending movement and MI device/patient retrieval, including moving inventory holders. Repositioning checks native path cost and has a hard 600-tick job expiry; it cannot remain protected indefinitely while walking a detour. NPC splinting and other selected device actions finish after one application. Evacuation selection requires free carrying hands, manipulation, sufficient movement and native carrying space; map exit requires the intended patient actually to be carried.

Generation observes completed gear generation and accepted pawn generation. Group enumeration is materialized once under a scoped generation context, then yielded in its original order. Only pawns with fresh gear events are provisioned; reused world pawns are never trained as new pawns. Existing items are counted, with no confiscation. Loading a save or changing settings grants nothing. Disabling stops new decisions/grants; current applications and carrying jobs can finish safely.

`NpcMedicalMemory` saves MI-only medic roles and drug history through a game component; it stores pawn references and primitive values, never third-party objects. Existing save keys are unchanged. New jobs and this component become part of saves; keep a backup before removing the mod mid-treatment. No migration or load-time replenishment is performed.

## Rescue before ordinary raid departure

Implemented, code-synced: 2026-10-20. `NpcDeparture` is the shared coordinator for the MI think node, the existing `Pawn_JobTracker.StartJob` admission guard and two scoped native-driver postfixes: `RimWorld.JobDriver_TakeAndExitMap.MakeNewToils` and `Verse.AI.JobDriver_Goto.MakeNewToils`. No setting, dependency, job definition or persistence key is added. The coordinator distinguishes medical work, bounded waiting, protected continuation, native release and an unhandled context.

Only Full survival participates. The exact native lord-job allowlist is `LordJob_AssaultColony` and `LordJob_Siege`; the exact phase allowlist is `LordToil_StealCover`, `LordToil_ExitMap` and `LordToil_ExitMapAndDefendSelf`. Quest-tagged lords, unknown/custom classes and panic fleeing retain native control. Forced orders, kidnapping, existing pawn carrying and recognized shuttle/transporter work are preserved. Native explosives, CE safety work and an individual's emergency medical withdrawal take priority. Modes 0/1 keep their existing departure behavior. Visitors, traders, caravans and other departure types are future scope, not supported departure takeovers.

Each helper discovers living, spawned, downed same-faction map pawns, prioritizing known raidmates without requiring current lord membership. Departure discovery does not use the ordinary 12/24-cell patient radius; native approach travel must fit the existing 2,500-tick medical budget. A helper assesses at most four distinct casualties per 150-tick interval, rotating the shortlist. Fresh native exit checks revalidate the current shortlist rather than expanding that interval's budget. Feasible emergency care, recovery-aware quick aid and existing local extraction precede strict off-map evacuation. Non-doctors gain carrying opportunities, never doctoring capability. Native patient and supply reservations distribute helpers without team roles or duplicate patient claims.

One weak, unsaved episode belongs to the departing lord. Its no-progress deadline is 1,250 ticks and its absolute deadline is 7,500 ticks; reconsideration uses 150 ticks. Only actual clinical improvement or useful transport toward the selected destination refreshes the no-progress deadline, and expired episodes cannot be revived by late progress. Job creation, claims, retries and waiting do not refresh it. Release remains latched for the episode despite repeated native assignments. A helper also releases when its assessed options are infeasible; other helpers may still use their capabilities. No new pickup starts after expiry or loss of Full survival. An already-carried patient may complete a safe transport. Loading creates a fresh bounded assessment; no episode or lease is saved.

Short native `Wait_Combat` jobs are allowed only from a known-safe waiting position for a bounded retry, the damage quiet window, another helper actively treating the patient, or remaining candidates in the bounded search. A quiet-window feasibility probe ignores fading impact history solely to establish whether waiting could help; committed evacuation always repeats the strict assessment. Unsafe/unknown routes, inaccessible patients and unavailable capability do not authorize indefinite waiting. Complete-route safety, close-hostile, impact, fire, explosive, thermal and access gates remain unchanged. These casualty-carrying gates do not block individual withdrawal under fire.

Prospective planning checks free-hand capacity without altering the carrying tracker. Carried loot is placed through native placement only after a feasible preview and an exclusive patient claim. Placement failure retains the cargo; successful placement is followed by a fresh actual assessment and reservation preflight. Failure releases only the proposed job's claims on their original map and preserves supplies. Equipment, ammunition, inventory contents and carried pawns are never discarded. Completed medical work returns to fresh native/coordinator selection instead of resuming stale theft.

The native wrappers preserve toil count/order and saved indices. They capture job ID, definition and reference before wrapping init, tick, interval and pre-action callbacks. Driver-owned finish/exit callbacks receive a final assessment without recursively starting a job inside cleanup; the next normal think/admission boundary starts prepared work. Other native toil cleanup and external finish callbacks remain intact. A replaced/recycled job cannot execute its old pickup/exit callbacks or release a replacement job's claims. Existing MI drivers recheck departure eligibility before pickup and final clinical consumption. Local extraction transfers its episode to immediate follow-up care. Scoped optional rescue ownership protects committed work/waits, then returns control after release rather than suppressing external rescue merely because Full survival is selected.

Development diagnostics require MI logging and development mode. Transition messages include mode, lord job/phase, duty, current captured job identity, cargo, medical capability and recent damage, with casualty discovery, reservation, access/travel, route rejection, wait and release reasons. These identify a particular incident's rejection sequence; the reported Empire incident cannot be attributed to one exact sequence without its runtime diagnostics.

## Mode-aware error fixes and evacuation safety

Implemented, code-synced: 2026-10-20. The mode values and existing job/settings/save identifiers are unchanged.

|---|---|---|---|
| 0: Medical actions only | Enabled, subject to care/drug settings | None | None |
| 1: Treatment-aware tactics | Enabled | Nearby repositioning and local casualty extraction | None |

`NpcMedicalDecision.Choose` is the production priority sequence. It completes the care pass across eligible patients before considering off-map evacuation in Full survival. Pain-downed casualties compare bounded quick aid, local extraction and deferral before committing; the same assessment runs after placement. Higher-priority physiological emergencies retain the ordinary stabilization path. Pain shock remains urgency 65. Ordinary incoming-fire gates remain intact, with a job-specific quick-aid exception described below. No standing state is forced after a dose. Other mods retain retreat/rescue decisions in lower modes, except for the existing protection of a committed local extraction.

`NpcMedicalAttempts.Attempt` captures the action and native job identity. Native CE observation holds an attempt handle; clearing or recycling the job cannot supply a null retry key, change its action or complete a replacement attempt. Completion is idempotent, and stage/progress callbacks require matching identity. Existing retry delays, dose budgets and health-progress criteria remain in force.

`NpcEvacuation` handles off-map carrying only. Selection checks the actor, patient, reservations, recent damage (600 ticks), recognized safety work and hostiles within eight cells. It evaluates both approach and carrying paths, starting the carrying assessment at the pathfinder's actual approach endpoint. Up to sixteen distinct candidates cover nearest edge projections and quarter/midpoint/three-quarter positions on all four edges; the least-cost safe candidate wins. `NpcExtractionAssessment` retains local extraction's bounded threat snapshot, but off-map assessments include hostile pawns and turrets across the map, without the local sixteen-threat cap. Per-assessment cell results are cached only for that synchronous assessment. This is conservative sight-based risk estimation, not a guarantee of future safety; it can defer routes despite distant enemies appearing harmless.

All off-map route cells must meet the existing known-safe threshold (`Worst < 0.1`). Optional risk APIs that cannot establish safety cause deferral. Fire, impacts, CE danger, temperature and access restrictions remain part of assessment. Full route checks run at pickup, every 60 ticks during travel and before exit; the next path cell is assessed immediately. No safe route means no MI off-map carry. Individual self-withdrawal retains its existing eligibility under fire, and local extraction retains its separate 600-tick planning budget and best-effort policy.

`JobDriver_NpcEvacuate` retains its four native toil positions and targets A/B. It normalizes pickup count to one, including saved jobs containing -1, revalidates capability and claims, supports the intended already-carried patient, and checks actual arrival before exit. Cleanup handles only that patient; failed placement retains it for native cleanup. Downgrades/disabling prevent new pickups, while an already-carried patient can complete a safe route. Local extraction also rejects new pickup after its required mode is disabled.

Known native explosives within the pinned nine-cell flee distance block competing MI decisions, including the CE adapter. Automatic treatment/transport yields through existing cleanup. Thin native movement/ingestion driver subclasses add yielding for MI repositioning, withdrawal and performance jobs while retaining their base toil layouts. Manual/player work is excluded. No new Harmony target or grenade scanner is added. Full grenade-avoidance behavior still depends on native scheduling and the complete mod chain.

Development diagnostics require MI logging to be enabled; all MI diagnostic levels respect that master switch. Diagnostics report changed selection/rejection states per actor/patient/stage, including mode, capability, reservations, incoming-fire/retry deferral, dose availability and route safety. Stable states are not logged every decision tick.

## Local casualty extraction

Implemented, code-synced: 2026-10-20. Treatment-aware tactics and Full survival now attempt local extraction of eligible downed patients before ordinary tending in an unsafe position. Medical actions only retains its movement policy. This is separate from Full survival's off-map evacuation: `MI_NpcExtract` carries a patient to nearby ground and hands care back locally; `MI_NpcEvacuate` retains its exit behavior. No kit, drug-balance, dependency or settings-key change is introduced.

Any eligible NPC combatant with free hands, manipulation, at least 50% movement and urgency below 70 may carry a patient; doctoring is not required for transport. Patient eligibility retains care flags, current hostility, medical restrictions and genuine incompatibilities. Native carrying space applies to patients, not the inventory mass/bulk checks used for medicine. Existing patient searches remain 12 cells, or 24 for medics. Confined prisoners and carried patients cannot use this movement to bypass confinement, doors, fences or allowed areas.

`NpcExtraction` screens ground destinations within 12 cells of the patient, then checks native paths for at most 16 candidates. Approach plus carrying has a 600-tick path-cost budget. Ranking considers worst normalized hazard, combined hazard, integrated route exposure and travel cost. Threats include nearby hostile pawns and turrets; CE's danger tracker contributes recent incoming fire. MI's existing damage callback maintains up to 64 impact locations per map for 600 ticks, including distant bombardment. Fire, explosive traps, access boundaries, both pawns' temperature tolerances and thermal illness also matter. Destinations and capabilities are revalidated; costly destination assessments are cached for 60 ticks during transport, while next-cell physical/fire boundaries are checked during movement. Native safety interruptions remain authoritative.

Recent harm is a reason to consider extraction, so transport does not use the ordinary medical quiet-window gate. Before a committed transport, an adjacent capable doctor may perform one indicated arrest/airway/critical-bleeding stabilization round with nearby supplies and an application estimate no longer than 600 ticks. Preparation is bounded per patient for 2,500 ticks; successful clinical pain and drug-dose rules are unchanged. If no transport can be issued, existing adjacent urgency-90 lifesaving care remains eligible. Ordinary tending is not substituted for extraction from an unsafe position.

A destination without demonstrable safety is a best-effort fallback. It must remain physically valid, must not be worse on the ranked hazards, and must offer at least a small combined-risk improvement; an already best-rated origin does not cause pointless movement. Unknown optional risk information never establishes safety. An uncertain/fallback carry is limited per patient to once per 2,500 ticks, regardless of rescuer; its allowance is consumed when carrying starts, not merely when a job is proposed. Arrival normally holds further relocation for 600 ticks. New impacts, nearby immediate threats, fire or severe thermal danger permit reassessment, while the fallback limit still applies.

`JobDriver_NpcExtract` has a stable toil layout and uses native targets A=patient, B=destination, C=origin. It reserves the patient and destination, rechecks pickup and route feasibility, carries exactly that patient, verifies actual placement, and offers one existing treatment to its medically capable carrier before releasing care to bounded triage. A non-doctor releases the patient for nearby help without gaining medical capability. Lost access, recovery, death, incapacity and hostility abort safely. Cleanup only places this job's selected carried patient nearby; it never destroys a patient, removes equipment or manipulates unrelated cargo. Placement failure leaves the patient intact for native cleanup. Switching modes or disabling the module permits a current safe carry to finish.

The approved `Pawn_JobTracker.StartJob` guard preserves carrying and native resumption only when an MI extraction's selected patient is carried and a recognized CE cover/hunker or Celsius shelter job takes over. The new extraction job is suspendable for this native resumption; ordinary `MI_NpcTend` remains non-suspendable. The guard checks the safety destination and route before retaining a confined patient. Other jobs, explicit orders and incapacitation retain native control. Its finalizer restores transient suspension state even if job startup throws. Existing ESP/NOLB hooks additionally protect committed extraction while the carrier remains viable, without taking over unrelated retreat/rescue decisions.

A verified safe arrival can release old-location medical deferral only while the patient remains there, both pawns' impact records remain older than arrival and current risk remains safe. New damage invalidates ordinary clearance. Best-effort arrival grants no such certificate; bounded quick aid uses its own fresh weighted assessment. Clinical care still validates physiology, dose effects, permissions, access and reservations; no persistent pain-dose cooldown or drug budget is added. Transport failures use the existing doctor/patient/job retry ledger, and successful placement counts as progress even if medical treatment remains outstanding. All extra planning, impact, preparation, fallback and clearance state is unsaved; active transport uses native job serialization.

## Incoming fire and failed medical attempts

Implemented, code-synced: 2026-10-20. The cover-then-retry policy applies in every NPC AI mode. Actual external ranged or explosive harm, including distant mortars with no nearby hostile pawn, starts an unsaved 600-tick quiet window on the injured pawn. `MoreInjuryComp.PostPostApplyDamage` forwards the existing callback after its existing injury handlers; no Harmony target or RNG call is added. Damage to either the doctor or intended patient defers ordinary retrieval and assistance. Feasible pocket self-care and adjacent (1.5-cell), urgency-90-or-higher lifesaving care remain candidates when no native safety action owns the doctor. Existing exposure, care, physiology, reservation and access checks still apply. These exceptions never override CE cover/hunkering.

The optional CE binding validates the owning assembly, public `CompSuppressable.isSuppressed` field, and float instance getters `CurrentSuppression` and `SuppressionThreshold`. It recognizes the `CE_JobDefOf.RunForCover` and `HunkerDown` definitions, with a validated CE-driver fallback for those names. It never calls the mutating `IsHunkering` getter. Unsupported suppression reads retain recognized job protection and the vanilla impact window, preserve external behavior, and issue one diagnostic. Both the MI think node and existing `CompTend` adapter honor safety ownership. The adapter also prevents native tactical tending from immediately bypassing a recent-impact or failed-tending deferral. Other CE tactical components and native suppression reactions remain active.

`MI_NpcTend` is explicitly non-suspendable, matching vanilla tending. `JobDriver_NpcTend` has one fixed toil graph for floor, inventory, already-carried and no-medicine routes, including self-care. Collection transfers one selected dose; application retains native tending effects. Access, exclusive patient availability, selected supply reservations, carrying space, effective mass/bulk and delivery feasibility are rechecked. Native finalization receives a final access/delivery check after the treatment wait. Active care keeps its original patient and selected supply through that application. Incapacitation, hostility, invalid targets, access loss and native safety interruptions can still abort it.

`NpcMedicalReservations.RestockSelected` generalizes unused-item cleanup to automatic MI medical devices and drugs as well as tending medicine. It returns only the selected item still carried by that job, up to available mass/bulk; unrelated cargo and equipment are untouched. Overflow keeps ordinary cleanup behavior. It does not recover floor items, replenish supplies or teleport them. Existing operations retain cleanup after toggling the module off. CE's native stabilization driver retains its own retrieval, consumption and cleanup semantics; MI recognizes its ownership and observes progress when control returns, without replacing or patching that driver.

`NpcMedicalAttempts` and `NpcMedicalRetry` hold weak, unsaved history per doctor, patient and medical job definition. Supply identity is excluded. An interruption, failed reservation/retrieval/application or apparent success without improvement delays unchanged attempts by 150, 300, 600, 1,200 and then at most 2,500 ticks. Progress is observed from actual tending, reduced condition severity/bleeding/pain or improved capacities; worsening alone is not progress. Death and resolved care do not accrue failures. The native CE observer cannot recover its exact completion condition and labels the handback accordingly; live scheduling remains an acceptance boundary.

Material indication/urgency, patient position, medical-care permission/relation, doctor capability or supply-source category changes permit immediate reconsideration. Equivalent floor stacks do not reset a deadline. Inventory/carry, patient inventory, another permitted inventory, ground and device-free treatment are distinct source categories. Emergency candidates are filtered before ranking, so a deferred action leaves independent treatments and other patients available. Clinical pain success remains effect-based: no pain-dose timestamp, successful-dose cooldown, performance allowance charge or epinephrine interval charge is introduced. Retry and impact history reset on load; active tending retains a stable resume layout.

Development mode logs selection-reason transitions, attempt stages and completion, including doctor/patient/job/supply identity, completion condition, actual progress, impact/suppression state and retry deadline. Equivalent repeated evaluations are not logged. No new settings keys, saved fields, dependencies, drug balancing or provisioning changes accompany this policy.

No capacity policy can guarantee a device for a group whose capable doctors cannot carry it. A rejected blood roll is not replaced with saline. Never shrink item stats merely to force a kit to fit; adjust kit quantities first. Weapons, armor and ammunition are never removed.

## Clinical pain care

Status: implemented. `NpcPainCare` owns assessment, willingness, selection and final checks; `NpcPainProjection` evaluates one additional dose; `NpcPainPolicy` holds the numeric decisions. These services use resolved runtime definitions for morphine, yayo, go-juice and the explicitly supported Det analgesics. Clinical reserves are separate from booster provisioning; existing-only blocks new clinical drugs as well. Clinical pain care can use permitted existing supplies independently of performance-use settings. Drug effects are unchanged. Ketamine and chloroform remain carried-but-not-automated.

Assessment reads native effective pain, `health.InPainShock`, `PainShockThreshold`, downed state, forced downing and relevant capacities. It distinguishes pain, other-cause and mixed collapse without promising that analgesia will restore standing. Pain immunity has no indication. Pain-only patients enter triage without tendable wounds. Physiological emergencies retain urgency 70–100, pain shock uses 65, impending collapse at 85% of threshold uses 55, and ordinary preventive relief uses 15. Both preventive levels require the configured relative trigger; pain shock remains eligible at every trigger setting. Pain shock separately qualifies as an emergency for the existing friendly/neutral/player care flags. Routine cross-faction pain maintenance remains excluded. Existing group preferences, current hostility, medical-care restrictions, prisoner self-care exception and 12/24-cell searches remain in force.

One-dose planning creates a detached `HediffSet`, shares existing read-only conditions, and replaces the candidate high and supported immediate linked effects with numeric `Hediff` previews. It does not construct drug comps, invoke `PostMake`, use the live severity setter, temporarily add a real condition, run ingestion, draw RNG or invoke the prognosis simulator. Native tolerance/body-size/gene effect calculation, pain calculation and capacity calculation operate on the projected dose/set. Low severity does not imply weaker analgesia: a same-stage top-up that only prolongs a high is rejected. Different analgesics retain multiplicative stacking; for example, active yayo's 0.5 factor and go-juice's 0.1 factor project to 0.05. Existing sufficient analgesia below the trigger needs no additional dose.

Projection rejects negligible relief, a life-threatening high, unsafe cumulative morphine buildup (0.4 or higher), dangerous worst-case ordinary overdose accumulation (0.5 or higher), and new or worsened incapacitating capacity failures. Consciousness uses the resolved native capacity boundary without an additional NPC floor; there is no 80% pre-dose consciousness requirement. Native capacity results must reproduce the current baseline; dependent capacities also receive a conservative bound for sedation because some workers consult the live capacity tracker. Unknown/random/custom ingestion outcomes and duplicate primary-effect outcomes fail closed for automatic clinical care. Morphine retains separate pre-dose safeguards: blood loss below 20%, no shock, and breathing and pumping at least 90%. Active ketamine, chloroform or alcohol prevents automatic analgesic combinations. Manual drug effects are unchanged.

The native overdose-eligibility helper and active chemical genes inform overdose risk; ordinary maximum severity is adjusted for body size, and the rare large-overdose chance is gene-modified. Immunity/dependency does not bypass projection of the drug's high, sedation or other risks. Addictiveness uses native tolerance statistics and the patient's gene factor. Acceptable doses rank projected recovery and useful function before physiological/bleeding risk, overdose, addiction, retrieval effort and market cost. Recovery requires projected pain below the individual threshold, capable essential capacities and no forced downing; mixed collapse receives no recovery bonus while another cause remains. For active patients, pain relief and normalized capacity gains must outweigh functional losses. Performance selection also uses positive projected net function instead of price-first selection. Native ingestion retains its actual random outcomes.
