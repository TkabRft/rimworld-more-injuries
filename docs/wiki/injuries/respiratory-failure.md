> **In-Game Description**
> _"**Respiratory failure** &mdash; Breathing has stopped. Restore a clear airway and functioning lungs. Unprotected cerebral hypoxia can kill in about 1.5 game hours, before the roughly three-hour respiratory failure timer."_

This condition is part of delayed vital failure, enabled by default for appropriate fleshy pawns, including animals and supported alien races and entities. Anatomy and native protections determine which failures apply. Creatures that do not require air do not develop respiratory failure from absent lungs. Progression varies modestly between updates. Animal shock, acidosis and broader trauma support remain outside this module.

**Treatment**: Restore the airway or functioning lungs. Existing CPR can clear choking on blood; this release does not provide mechanical ventilation.

Restoring the underlying function removes the failure. Existing [hypoxic injury](/docs/wiki/injuries/hypoxia.md#hypoxia) and [lasting brain damage](/docs/wiki/injuries/brain-damage.md#brain-damage) require their own recovery or treatment. Removing all structurally surviving consciousness sources remains immediately fatal.

To move an existing Death Rattle save to More Injuries, follow the [save transition instructions](/docs/wiki/save-compatibility.md#death-rattle-transition). Keep Death Rattle enabled until the transition succeeds and a new copy has been saved.

The failure itself still progresses toward death over about three game hours, but complete unprotected [cerebral hypoxia](/docs/wiki/injuries/hypoxia.md#cerebral-hypoxia) can become terminal in about 1.5 game hours. Brain injury can therefore limit the rescue window first. Existing native death protections remain authoritative.
