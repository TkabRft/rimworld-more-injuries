A medically induced coma limits consciousness to 10% and **halves new acute hypoxic brain injury**. It buys time for treatment of [cardiac arrest](/docs/wiki/injuries/cardiac-arrest.md#cardiac-arrest), [respiratory failure](/docs/wiki/injuries/respiratory-failure.md#respiratory-failure) or severe [shock](/docs/wiki/injuries/hypovolemic-shock.md#hypovolemic-shock). It does not restore circulation or oxygen, remove existing injury, or slow shock, acidosis and organ-failure timers. Native vacuum exposure and decompression remain dangerous.

## Induction and Reversal

Schedule the [operation](/docs/wiki/surgeries.md#artificial-coma) with **five industrial or glitterworld medicine** and medical care allowing that medicine. Herbal medicine cannot be used. The procedure takes 400 work and follows native surgery restrictions. The patient must be eligible to receive Wake-up; native mutant drug restrictions still apply.

One normal dose of Wake-up reverses the induced state, through an awakening operation or normal drug administration. Its ordinary effects and risks apply. Awakening does not cure natural [coma](/docs/wiki/injuries/coma.md#coma), [brain damage](/docs/wiki/injuries/brain-damage.md#brain-damage) or the initiating emergency.

## Prolonged Coma

Severity grows by ten percentage points per game day. Risky stages begin at 70%, and above 72% prolonged coma can produce lasting neurological complications. At 100% the condition is lethal unless native death protection prevents it. Re-induction does not reset severity or stack the protective effect. Protection against new oxygen injury does not cancel the coma's own complications.
